# Dashboard Guide

## Overview

The dashboard presents business performance metrics generated after evaluating different pricing strategies.

---

# Dashboard Sections

## Revenue Summary

Displays

- Total Revenue
- Average Revenue
- Revenue Growth

---

## Inventory Performance

Displays

- Inventory Utilization
- Sell-through Rate

---

## Pricing Performance

Displays

- Average Selling Price

---

## Policy Ranking

Ranks

- Static Pricing
- Rule-Based Pricing
- Q-Learning
- DQN

based on Total Revenue.
