# Human-in-the-Loop Feedback Learning

## Objective

Allow a hotel revenue manager to review AI-generated pricing decisions.

## Workflow

AI Agent

↓

Suggested Price

↓

Manager Decision

↓

Approve / Modify / Reject

↓

Feedback Memory

↓

Preference Learning

↓

Improved Pricing Policy

---

## Benefits

- Explainable AI
- Human supervision
- Continuous learning
- Better trust
- Business-friendly deployment

---

## Future Improvements

- Online learning
- Multi-manager feedback
- Feedback confidence score
- Reinforcement from historical decisions

---

## Conclusion

Human feedback improves pricing quality while maintaining business control.
